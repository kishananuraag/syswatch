# T20 — Nested layers + polish + hover + temps + 15 s powermetrics

## Powermetrics efficiency (Kishan's <2 % target)

- Bump `powermetrics.interval_ms` from 5000 to **15000** (15 s). Timeline shows
  15 s resolution for recent data. powermetrics spikes drop from every 5 s to
  every 15 s. Total CPU should land ~1.5–2 %.
- Config key: `powermetrics.interval_ms = 15000` in collector config + daemon arg.
- Coverage line updates to "sampling every 15 s".

## Layer 1 / 2 / 3 (nested, inline expansion — NOT separate views, NOT drawers)

**L1 (90% — one viewport, glanceable):**
- 6 vital chips in a row: number + sparkline + delta. ONE line of meta max.
- 3 small tiles: Network, Disk, Thermal. Each: number + sparkline + coverage.
- NO wide chart. NO text walls. NO findings. NO measures.
- Hover a chip → tooltip with a 30 px mini chart (last 60 s) + source line.
  No click needed for a quick look.

**L2 (99% — expands in place, no drawer, no slide-over):**
- Click a chip → it expands inline, pushing content down. The rest of L1
  dims slightly (opacity 0.6) but stays visible. No context loss.
- Expanded chip shows: full horizontally-scrollable chart + measures (mean/peak/p95)
  + top consumers (5 rows) + coverage line. Drag-to-zoom on the chart.
- Click again or click another chip → collapses.

**L3 (1% — deep detail, expands inside L2):**
- Inside the expanded L2, a "details" toggle reveals: findings block,
  records feed, per-core breakdown, full SMC sensor list, peak moments.
- L3 is collapsed by default. Most users never open it.

**Knowledge page moves to ⚙ settings.** It's reference material, not a layer.

## Temps as first-class module

- Thermal tile (L1): CPU die °C + GPU °C + pressure. Sparkline of CPU temp.
- L2 expansion: full SMC sensor list (CPU cores, GPU, SSD, battery) with
  °C values, plus a temp-over-time chart (from root sample history).
- L3: raw SMC key table (key, label, value, unit).

## Less text, more numbers

- Every tile: number (big) + sparkline + one meta line (10 px, muted) + delta.
- No coverage line on L1 (moves to L2).
- No findings on L1 (moves to L3).
- No "measures" table on L1 (moves to L2).
- Top consumers: L1 shows 3 rows max (name + share %). Full list in L2.

## Horizontal scroll on ALL charts

- L1 sparklines: fixed width (no scroll, 60 s window).
- L2 full charts: `overflow-x: auto`, min-width 720, custom 6 px scrollbar.
- L3 detail charts: same.

## Hover-to-reveal

- `mouseenter` on a chip → show tooltip (positioned below the chip) with:
  - 30 px tall canvas: last 60 s of that metric
  - Source line (10 px): "host_processor_info · 1 s"
- `mouseleave` → hide after 200 ms delay (so you can move to the tooltip).
- Tooltip has `pointer-events: none` (no interaction needed).
